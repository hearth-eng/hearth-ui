1. Overview

We want every booking on Hearth to feel fair, from cancellation through to completion. This policy explains when you're eligible for a refund, how much, and how long it takes.

2. Cancellation Before Service

  • More than 2 hours before the slot: full refund to your original payment method, or instant credit to your Hearth Wallet.
  • Within 2 hours of the slot: a small convenience fee may be deducted to compensate the assigned Professional for lost time.

3. Cancellation After Professional Assignment

If a Professional has already been dispatched or has arrived at your location, cancelling the booking may incur a visit charge to cover their travel time, in addition to any convenience fee above.

4. Service Quality Guarantee & Re-work

If the completed job does not meet the standard described at booking, report it through the app within 24 hours with details or photos. We will first offer a free re-service by the same or another Professional. If a re-service cannot resolve the issue, a partial or full refund will be issued based on the nature of the shortfall.

5. Refund Method & Timeline

  • Refunds to your Hearth Wallet are instant and can be used for your next booking.
  • Refunds to your original payment method (card, UPI, net banking) typically reflect within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank.

6. Non-Refundable Situations

  • Change of mind after a job has been fully completed to the agreed scope.
  • Issues arising from inaccurate information provided at booking (e.g. wrong address, incorrect job scope).
  • Cost of materials or parts already purchased on your behalf, once purchased.

7. Rescheduling vs Refunds

If your plans change, rescheduling is usually faster than cancelling and rebooking, and avoids any convenience fee when done more than 2 hours ahead. You can reschedule directly from the Bookings tab in the app.

8. How to Request a Refund

  1. Open the relevant booking in the Hearth app or website.
  2. Select "Report an issue" and choose the reason that applies.
  3. Add details or photos where relevant — this speeds up review.
  4. Our support team reviews most requests within 24 hours.

9. Contact Us

Still have a question about a specific refund? Reach our support team at help@gethearth.example or via our Contact page, and reference your booking ID.
