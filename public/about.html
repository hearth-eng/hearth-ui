Our story

Built by people who show up.

Hearth started with a simple frustration: finding someone reliable to fix a leaking tap or deep-clean an apartment shouldn't take three phone calls and a leap of faith. So we built a place where every professional is checked, trained and rated — and every customer knows exactly what they're paying for, before they book.

Why we exist

Home services in most cities run on word-of-mouth and luck — a neighbour's recommendation, a number scrawled on a pamphlet, a stranger who may or may not turn up. We thought homes deserved better than luck.

Hearth was founded in Bengaluru with one rule: nobody gets listed unless we'd trust them in our own home. That meant building a verification process from scratch, training every professional on the specifics of their craft, and being upfront about pricing before a single tool comes out of the bag.

Today, thousands of professionals earn a steady, respected livelihood through Hearth, and hundreds of thousands of households trust us with the small and large jobs that keep a home running.
